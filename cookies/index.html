Cookies

Simple choices about website data.

This website should use only the cookies and technologies that are genuinely configured. Do not list analytics or marketing tools that have not been installed.

Essential technologies

Essential storage may be used for security, accessibility, navigation, form protection and remembering necessary preferences. These functions cannot always be disabled without affecting the website.

Analytics

Analytics should be disabled by default unless a compliant tool is configured and the required consent mechanism is active. The deployment README identifies where configuration must be completed.

Marketing

No advertising or behavioural marketing technology should be enabled unless it is intentionally installed, documented and covered by valid consent.

Third-party content

WhatsApp, email, video, maps, scheduling or payment links may place their own cookies after the user chooses to open them. Their privacy information applies separately.

Managing choices

Users can change browser settings, delete stored data and use the website’s consent control when one is enabled. Blocking essential storage may affect functionality.
