Privacy

How student information is handled.

This privacy notice is a practical website draft for Ashlyn A Merrigan Cursos and should be reviewed before publication for full LGPD compliance.

Controller: ASHLYN A MERRIGAN CURSOS, CNPJ 46.304.161/0001-70, Londrina, Paraná. Contact: nativeielts@gmail.com.

Information collected

  • Names, contact details, nationality and current location.
  • Language level, study goals, dates, availability and programme choices.
  • Enrolment, timetable, attendance, assessment and portfolio records.
  • Payment, invoice and transaction information.
  • School-document requests and official refusal evidence when a refund policy requires it.
  • Accessibility information voluntarily provided for support.
  • Website technical, cookie and security information.

Why it is used

Information is used to respond to enquiries, assess programme fit, prepare offers, create student records, teach and assess the course, issue accurate school documents, process payments, prevent fraud, comply with legal duties and protect legitimate interests.

Sensitive documents

Do not send passport copies through EdBot, an unsecured form or an unverified message. Sensitive documents are requested only when necessary through the method stated by the school.

Sharing

Information may be shared only as reasonably necessary with authorised teachers, administrative providers, accountants, payment providers, technology services or public authorities under a lawful requirement. The school does not sell student data.

Retention

Records are kept for the period required by educational, contractual, tax, accounting, legal and legitimate record-keeping needs. Unsuccessful enquiry data should not be kept longer than reasonably necessary.

International services

Email, cloud, video, analytics or messaging providers may process information outside Brazil. Suitable contractual and security measures should be reviewed before launch.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, a person may request confirmation, access, correction, information about sharing, review of certain automated decisions, portability where applicable, anonymisation, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data, and withdrawal of consent where consent is the basis.

Security and incidents

Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards are used, but no online system is risk-free. Suspected privacy incidents should be reported promptly to the contact email.

Updates

This notice may be updated when the website, forms, providers or legal requirements change.
