Culture as coursework

Attend, observe, use the language, reflect.

Cultural activities are not simply excursions. The teacher prepares a language objective and evidence task before the activity, then uses homework and discussion afterwards to turn the experience into learning.

  • Prepare key vocabulary and respectful questions.
  • Observe language, behaviour, signs, services and context.
  • Separate what you saw from what you assume.
  • Complete a reflection, comparison, interview, map, photo essay or presentation.
  • Receive language and intercultural feedback.
