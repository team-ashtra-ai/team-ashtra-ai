About the school

Language learning built around real life.

ASH-TRA Languages Brazil is the public-facing language programme of Ashlyn A Merrigan Cursos, a Brazilian language-teaching company based in Londrina, Paraná.

Why this programme exists

Integration should feel possible.

Moving to a new country can make ordinary tasks feel difficult. The programme was created to connect language study with the moments that shape daily life: understanding people, asking for help, navigating services, building friendships and learning how Brazil works.

Students are not treated as document files or passive tourists. They are learners building practical independence, cultural awareness and a meaningful relationship with the country.

S

The programme combines structured language teaching, practical communication and Brazilian cultural learning. Students receive a written programme offer identifying their language, dates, timetable, weekly workload, delivery format and tuition before enrolment is completed.

School information explains the educational programme clearly. Immigration decisions remain the responsibility of the competent Brazilian authority, and independent legal advice may be sought where needed.

Who it is for

Serious students, not paper enrolments.

The programme is for international students who genuinely intend to attend, learn and participate. It is not suitable for anyone seeking school documents without real study.
