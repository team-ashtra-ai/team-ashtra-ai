## Editorial safeguards

- Dates, opening hours, event schedules, travel conditions and immigration requirements can change and should be rechecked before publication updates.
- Articles do not promise visa approval, a specific language level, employment, financial return or a guaranteed personal outcome.
- Educational language and cultural support is distinguished from legal, medical and emergency professional advice.
- Hero images are original SVG illustrations created for this website package; no third-party photographs are bundled.
