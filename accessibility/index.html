Website accessibility

The website uses semantic headings, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus, sufficient contrast, responsive layouts, text alternatives for meaningful images and reduced-motion support.

Learning adjustments

Students may request reasonable support such as additional processing time, readable digital formats, transcripts or captions where appropriate, reduced-copying alternatives, mobility-aware cultural tasks and an equivalent online or classroom activity when an off-site task is not accessible.

Privacy and dignity

Students are not required to disclose more personal information than is necessary to discuss a support request. Immigration status, health history, religion, politics, family history and trauma are not used as forced classroom topics.

Request support

Contact nativeielts@gmail.com with the page, material or learning barrier you encountered and the type of adjustment that may help.

Limits

Third-party payment, video, map, messaging and booking services may have their own accessibility limitations. We will offer a reasonable alternative when one is available.
