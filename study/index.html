Study formats

Flexible access, honest definitions.

The programme combines live teaching, practical communication and cultural learning through the format agreed with each student.

01

Online

Live lessons plus city-based or media-based cultural tasks that can be completed where the student lives.

02

Hybrid

Online academic instruction with selected in-person cultural or practical sessions when confirmed.

03

Face to face

Live classes and guided cultural immersion that connect language learning with everyday life in Brazil.

12+

What shorter enrolments cover

Students choosing 12–39 weeks follow the programme from the appropriate assessed entry point. Their offer identifies the units, outcomes and assessment included. A shorter course does not guarantee the full B1 outcome.

✓

What completion requires

Suggested completion requires at least 70% overall and a minimum 75% attendance under school policy. Other authorities may apply different rules. Homework supports academic recovery but does not convert an absence into attendance.
