The complete pathway

From first words to independent participation.

The 40-week core contains twenty two-week units. Each week includes five scheduled three-hour classes, combining core language, pronunciation, listening, reading, writing, culture, real-world tasks and feedback.

  • Beginner-friendly entry from Pre-A1
  • Term reviews at Weeks 10, 20 and 30
  • Unit checks and learning portfolio
  • Final integrated assessment in Week 40
  • Optional extension through Week 50
